# AIShop iPhone App: Vision Roadmap

Each sprint must remain independently demonstrable with local fixtures. Sprint
plans and their later task documents follow root governance and approval gates.

| Sprint | Increment | Demonstrable outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 001 | Candidate target signal | One reference image is found provisionally in streamed video |
| 002 | Candidate regions | Small shelf targets are localized and compared as crops |
| 003 | Quality gate | Blur, glare, framing, distance, and motion produce guidance |
| 004 | Tracking and deduplication | Repeated observations become one stable item episode |
| 005 | Barcode and OCR evidence | Codes and readable package text strengthen identity |
| 006 | Local catalog matching | Multiple catalog targets receive ranked candidates |
| 007 | Evidence fusion | Visual, text, code, and temporal facts yield calibrated states |
| 008 | Price association | Shelf labels are paired with product regions and uncertainty |
| 009 | Produce mode | Bins, signs, PLUs, category, variety, and quality cues are handled |
| 010 | Review and correction | Shopper confirmations and corrections become labeled evidence |
| 011 | Server clarification | Bounded evidence packages resolve uncertain local candidates |
| 012 | Physical-device validation | Live camera, latency, thermal, and battery behavior are measured |

## Product expansion after the first slice

Shopping-list finding is the entry point. The same evidence model later supports
problem-solving, substitutes, opportunity detection, cross-store observations,
and personal product memory without mixing identity with preference or price.
