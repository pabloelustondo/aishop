# Sprint 006 — SDLC2 Retrofit of VISTA-Originated Controls

HumanReviewerInitials:

## Goal

AI Shop is the SDLC2 showcase and must carry the methodology's latest
controls. VISTA adopted SDLC2, then strengthened it in practice. This
documentation-only governance sprint retrofits those proven
strengthenings into AI Shop's canonical governance.

## In scope

1. Work-item authorization: explicit pointer file, never-select-by-
   recency, one component per task, immutable Tasks-bound prompt
   (path + SHA-256), and the READY lifecycle states.
2. Exact-hash approval identity: SHA-256 in every approval handoff,
   staged activation-envelope capture and carry-through, and the
   recorded one-time approval-transcription pattern.
3. Independent review and disposition: findings-first reviews with
   Blocker/Major/Minor, gate-by-gate assessment, fixed decision
   vocabularies, reviewer-recommends/human-decides, per-action
   authority table, and the bounded correction loop.
4. Materialize AI Shop copies of the seven VISTA artifact templates
   with the template-source blank-placeholder rule.
5. An AI Shop next-work-item pointer and execute skill.
6. Governance README routing lines for every new rule document.

## Non-goals

Do not narrow AI Shop's all-Markdown review scope, import VISTA product
invariants, alter Sprints 001–005 records, weaken the 50-line rule, or
change any executable behavior. Draft rule documents accompany this
plan as non-authoritative candidates until approved.

## Sequencing and acceptance

Plan approval authorizes drafting Sprint 006 Tasks only. Execute after
Sprint 005 merges to avoid README conflicts. Accepted when every scoped
control is a routed, approved, ≤50-line governance document.
