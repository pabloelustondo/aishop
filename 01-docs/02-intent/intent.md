# AI Shop — Project Intent

HumanReviewerInitials:PME

AI Shop helps people shop wisely across different supermarkets.

The prototypical user is Pablo. He has four nearby supermarkets, and no single store is best for every product. Prices, quality, and product availability vary between them. Because he usually shops on foot and cannot visit every store on every trip, deciding what to buy—and where—can be confusing.

AI Shop will maintain a catalog of the products Pablo normally buys. While shopping, it should help him judge whether a product is a good choice at that store based on its price and quality. It should also warn him when buying the product elsewhere would be wiser.

The initial goal is simple: help Pablo buy the products he likes, at good quality and a good price, without needing to visit every nearby supermarket each time.
