# Benchmark 02 — Human Product Identification

HumanReviewerInitials:PME

## Benchmark

Without AI Shop, Pablo identifies all the products in front of him by looking at them himself.

He visually separates the products, reads the visible labels and packaging, recognizes the brands and product types, and builds his own understanding of what is present.

## Strengths

- Pablo can use his own judgment and surrounding context.
- No application, server, or network connection is required.

## Limitations

- Identifying several products takes attention and time.
- Products can be missed in a cluttered scene.
- Small, obscured, or unclear labels can be difficult to read.
- Pablo must organize and remember the findings himself.

## Comparison for AI Shop

A better AI Shop report should identify the visible products clearly and together, while indicating anything it cannot identify confidently. It should make the scene faster and easier to understand than unaided visual inspection.
