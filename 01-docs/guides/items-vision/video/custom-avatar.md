# VISTA Presenter — Pablo

## Intent
Create an approximate, recognisable likeness of Pablo for the narrated presentation.
Reference: the profile photo Pablo supplied, also used on LinkedIn and his résumé.
Keep the familiar smile, tousled hair, navy blazer, and open white shirt.
Use light stylisation and natural facial character.

## Google Vids source
The custom-avatar builder used here accepts a description, not a portrait upload.
The supplied photo informed the description; it was not uploaded through a logo field.
Custom avatar name: `Pablo — VISTA`.
Voice: `Persuader`, engaging and low pitch, matching the original narration.
The avatar is saved within the existing VISTA Google Vids document.

## Initial direction
Friendly middle-aged man with light warm skin, an oval face, brown eyes,
a prominent straight nose, natural forehead lines and smile lines.
Thick, casually tousled dark brown hair with grey temples and short grey sides.
Very light salt-and-pepper stubble, a broad warm smile, and no glasses.
Navy blazer, white open-collar shirt, no tie, and a clean light-grey background.
Chest-up framing, direct camera gaze, soft light, and realistic facial proportions.

## Refinement
Use a slightly longer, narrower face with a less square jaw.
Make eyebrows less heavy and symmetrical, and hair less sculpted.
Reduce the beard to faint stubble and retain a slightly asymmetric smile.
The result is an interpretation, not an exact photographic likeness.

## Placement
Use the custom presenter for the introduction and the technical scene's inset.
Keep the inset clear of diagram labels, arrows, and the slide footer.
Preserve scripts and existing public viewing permissions.
