# Intent

## Speaker notes

Human attention is the constrained resource in this project.

Agents can produce more text than anyone can review.

A presentation is the answer when something must be understood, not just filed.

The form is always the same, so the effort goes into the content.

The slide carries one meaning that works on a screen.

The notes explain it in words short enough to be spoken.

The fine print links to whatever proves or details the claim.

A reader who never opens the fine print still ends with the right understanding.

Not everything deserves a presentation. Use one where human judgement matters.

A presentation informs a decision. It never makes one, and it authorises nothing.
