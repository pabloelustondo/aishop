# Anatomy of a presentation

## Speaker notes

A presentation is a folder. Its name says what it is about.

The index at the top lists every slide and its notes, in order.

The index also says the status and what the presentation describes.

Each slide is a numbered folder, so slides sort in presentation order.

Leave the numbers two digits wide. Insert a slide by renumbering, not with letters.

The first slide is always the title and subtitle of the thing being explained.

After that, the type decides the order. An untyped presentation chooses its own.

Evidence that slides cite may live in the presentation, under artifacts.

A generator, an export to a slide program, or a check script are all optional.

Plain files are enough. Nothing here requires tooling.

Where a presentation lives in the repository is decided by its type.
