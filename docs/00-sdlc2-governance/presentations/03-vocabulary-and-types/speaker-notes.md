# Vocabulary and types

## Speaker notes

Two words now have fixed meanings.

"Presentation" is the parent. It means the form: slides, notes, fine print.

"Walkthrough" is a type. It means the specific deck made when a sprint awaits review.

When Pablo asks for a walkthrough, follow the walkthrough rule and this one.

When he asks for a presentation, follow this rule, and find out the purpose.

Do not assume a presentation is a walkthrough. Do not add a walkthrough's required slides to it.

Two further types have names but no rule yet.

An agent asked for one of those follows the shared form and says plainly that the type is undefined.

It does not invent the type's rules. Defining a type is Pablo's decision.

An untyped presentation is allowed. This folder is one.

Its title-slide notes must then say who it is for and what question it answers.
