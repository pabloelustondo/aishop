# What a walkthrough must cover

## Speaker notes

Coverage is judged on these eight kinds of element, not on files.

A deck is complete when every element the sprint introduced appears on a slide.

Components are the named parts of the design, whatever files they live in.

Interfaces are described in words. A Swift or TypeScript signature is fine print.

Every scenario the acceptance criteria name deserves a flow.

Data contracts matter most where a record crosses a boundary: a log, a report, an API.

Anything with a lifecycle gets a state diagram.

Rules without reasons cannot be reviewed, only obeyed. Always give the reason.

Boundaries say what may depend on what: package and app, test-only code, debug-only code.

Failure behaviour is part of the design. Say what a failure does, not only what success does.

Several elements may share a slide when they are small. One dense element may need two slides.

As a guide, a component whose source runs past about a hundred lines earns a second slide.
