# Intent 02 — Better Product Reports

HumanReviewerInitials:PME

## Intent

After analyzing a product, AI Shop should present the result in a clear, dedicated report instead of fitting the complete answer into the camera header.
Ideally, it should presenta a report with ALL the products recognized and a list of productgs not recognized.
Ideally the app should be able to identify all "products" in general, even if it cannot determine teh actual specific product.

## Why

Sprint 001 proved that the app can recognize products and return useful information. It also showed that a short area over the camera can truncate the result and make it difficult to understand.

## Direction

The report should be quick to scan and should not feel like a chat conversation. It should clearly communicate what product was identified, what the app observed, what conclusion it reached, and when important information is missing.

Pablo should be able to understand the result and then return easily to the camera to analyze another product.

The exact report contents and interaction will be defined in the Sprint 002 use case and proof of concept.
