# Benchmark 01 — Current Manual Shopping Assistance

## Current baseline

Today, Pablo can use his iPhone and ChatGPT to decide whether a product in a supermarket is a good buy.

He can either:

1. Take a picture with the iPhone camera, open ChatGPT, upload it, and ask for feedback.
2. Open ChatGPT, use its camera directly, capture the product, and ask for feedback.

Pablo then supplies relevant information, reads the response, and continues the conversation when the first answer is not enough.

## What works today

ChatGPT can provide useful feedback about a photographed product. With the right context, it can help assess quality, price, preferences, and whether the product is a good buy.

The current process is therefore a reasonable functional alternative. AI Shop must provide more than image analysis or access to a chatbot.

## Current friction

Pablo may need to:

- move between the camera, photo storage, and ChatGPT;
- upload or capture the image;
- explain what he wants; and
- read or continue a conversation before reaching a decision.

ChatGPT also lacks an organized, persistent shopping context containing:

- what Pablo currently needs;
- what he normally buys and prefers;
- prices seen at different supermarkets; and
- relevant product and store history.

## Standard AI Shop must beat

For the same product decision, AI Shop should:

- require fewer actions;
- capture and analyze the product in one continuous experience;
- use relevant shopping context without Pablo recreating it;
- minimize typing, reading, and conversational back-and-forth; and
- present a clear recommendation faster and at a glance.

If AI Shop requires the same image handling, context entry, and conversation as the current process, it has not improved on the benchmark.
