# 005 - Visual Source

## Core message
AI Shop is both a working application and a practical SDLC2 case study.
The twelve steps connect the business problem to architecture, implementation, operation, and learning.

## Composition
Use a 1600 by 900 canvas with the established light background and dark navy text.
Place the title at the upper left with the short teal rule used by the existing deck.
Below the title, draw one continuous horizontal lifecycle across four phase regions.
Avoid a dashboard or collection of disconnected cards.

## Phase structure
- Understand: steps 1 through 3.
- Evaluate: steps 4 through 6.
- Build: steps 7 through 9.
- Operate and Learn: steps 10 through 12.

Each phase has a quiet colour field, a phase label, and three numbered step labels.
A single line connects all twelve numbered markers from left to right.
Add a restrained return line from step 12 toward step 1 to show continued learning.

## Exact step labels
1. Business and Technical Context
2. Intent
3. Use Cases and System Model
4. Benchmarks and Test Strategy
5. High-Level Architecture
6. Working Proof of Concept
7. Planning
8. Low-Level Design and Specifications
9. Build, Code, and Test
10. Deployment, Infrastructure, and Operational Reality
11. Review, Human QA, and Release
12. Observability and Learning

## Closing statement
AI Shop demonstrates the application and the evidence used to evaluate its architecture.

## Output
Generate `005-visual.svg` as an editable 16:9 SVG.
Keep all labels as SVG text and preserve the full twelve-step wording.
