# 03 - What AI Shop Must Improve

Benchmarks begin with the useful processes people already have.
An AI response alone does not establish that AI Shop improves those processes.
The repository documents three practical baselines.

## Manual shopping assistance
A shopper can already photograph a product and ask ChatGPT for advice.
That process can consider quality, price, preferences, and purchase context.
Its friction comes from moving between tools and repeatedly explaining the situation.
AI Shop should require fewer actions and reuse relevant shopping context.
The recommendation should become clear faster and with less reading or typing.

## Human product identification
A person can inspect labels, packaging, and surrounding context directly.
Human judgment needs no application, server, or network connection.
Dense scenes still take time and can hide small or obscured products.
AI Shop should organise visible products while declaring uncertain identification.

## Manual shelf inspection and review
An inspector can photograph a shelf and record observations in notes or a spreadsheet.
A second person can compare those findings with the original photograph.
The manual process becomes difficult when evidence, findings, and corrections separate.
AI Shop should preserve one traceable relationship across those materials.
Reviewer identity, decisions, corrections, and timing should remain attributable.

## Benchmark principle
The application should reduce manual effort without replacing human authority.
Each product use case needs its own comparison with the process it intends to improve.
AI Shop shopping support and VISTA inspection therefore share methods but not one score.

## Evidence boundary
These documents define comparison standards rather than measured acceptance thresholds.
The presentation should not claim that AI Shop already beats every baseline.
Later slides will show current recognition evidence and the remaining verification work.

## Visual direction
Show three existing human workflows feeding one evaluation question.
Make the outcome about reduced effort, preserved evidence, and human authority.
Avoid model names and implementation details on this slide.
