# Use Case 01 — Evaluate a Product in a Store

## Purpose

Help Pablo decide whether a product he sees while shopping is a good or a bad buy.

## Primary actor

Pablo, shopping in one of his nearby supermarkets with the AI Shop iPhone app.

## Context

Pablo may already have an idea of what he wants to buy. AI Shop also maintains a grocery list representing products he currently needs. How that list is created will be defined later.

Pablo often buys the same kinds of products, but he is free to consider and buy anything that interests him while walking through the store.

## Main flow

1. Pablo walks through a supermarket aisle and notices a product, such as tomatoes in the produce section.
2. He points the iPhone camera at the product and captures an image with AI Shop.
3. AI Shop analyzes the visible product, including available evidence about its price and quality.
4. AI Shop considers what it knows about:
   - the products Pablo currently needs;
   - the products Pablo normally buys;
   - current or previously observed prices at this and other nearby supermarkets; and
   - the apparent quality of the product.
5. AI Shop gives Pablo a simple recommendation, such as:
   - this is a good buy;
   - this is too expensive;
   - the apparent quality is not good; or
   - buying this product at another supermarket would be wiser.
6. Pablo decides whether to buy the product.

## Outcome

The iPhone acts as a second eye for Pablo, helping him make a better in-store decision using both what the camera can see and what AI Shop already knows about his shopping needs, preferences, and nearby supermarkets.

AI Shop advises Pablo; Pablo always makes the final buying decision.

## Outside this use case

This use case does not yet define how the grocery list, usual-product catalog, price history, or store information is created and maintained.
