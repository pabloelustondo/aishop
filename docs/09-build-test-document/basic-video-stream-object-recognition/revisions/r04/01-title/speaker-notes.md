# Streaming video candidate recognition

## Speaker notes

Welcome to A I Shop Vision, the foundation built in Sprint One. This walkthrough is for Pablo and future maintainers who need to understand the design and check its evidence.

Four short chapters explain the purpose and architecture, frame analysis, episodes and evidence, and verification on Mac and iPhone. Keep the clickable companion open when you want to inspect code or tests.

We describe the September twentieth working tree based on commit ninety A five F eight B. The system analyzes prerecorded video for one banana reference. Live camera capture and general object detection remain outside this sprint. A physical iPhone demonstration and acceptance are still pending.
